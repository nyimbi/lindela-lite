### §4 remainder — ENH-07 delivered, and the largest single win here

`read(collections[])` had been implemented in the store for months with **no
caller**, so every request materialised the whole store: 143 MB to serve a page
of incidents. The status documents called the item done; the benefit was zero.

The manifest is now **measured** for 82 of 170 documented routes, by two passes
in `scripts/collect-route-manifests.mjs`: a probe that drives every route
against a real server with the read instrumented, and a source closure over each
handler family's transitive call graph. The second pass is not optional — the
probe alone measures the *rejection* path of every body-validating handler, which
is how `POST /api/v1/reports` was briefly narrowed to two collections and took
thirteen tests down with it. The other 88 keep today's whole-store read, named
with which of the two reasons applies.

`JsonStore.read` now honours a manifest as `PostgresStore.read` already did,
which is what makes the mechanism testable at all. Measured on 39,696 records:
**14 ms against 110 ms**, 31× against the old spine.
