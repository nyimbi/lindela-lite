# Cognitive walkthrough — Lindela Lite

Five real tasks, performed against the running product on `:4177`, driven over CDP against the Chrome on `:9333`. Each row records the four walkthrough questions for a first-time user:

1. **Goal** — will the user try to achieve this?
2. **Notice** — will they notice the correct action is available?
3. **Associate** — will they connect action to effect?
4. **Progress** — will they see that something changed?

Severity: `blocker` (task cannot be completed), `major` (completed, but the user is misled or lacks the information to be confident), `minor` (friction), `observation` (works, worth recording).

Everything below was observed in the product. Where I inferred something about mechanism I say so and mark it as such. Screenshots were written to `/tmp/cw/` during the walk.

No figure on the card, in the modal, or in the result | major |
| 2.3 | Sanity-check the trigger against the protocol | Confirm the threshold | Compared cards to `Active pre-authorised protocols` on the same page | pass | pass | **fail** | — | Cards say `48 against a threshold of 40` for Bor and `61 against a threshold of 40` for Aweil. | 2.4 | Approve card 1 (Bor) | Commit the decision | Clicked `Approve`, modal appeared, clicked `Approve` in the modal | pass | pass | **fail** | **fail** | Modal reads `Approve workflow?` with a `Reason` dropdown (`Confirmed threat` / `Pre-authorized protocol` / `Manual override`). | 2.5 | Approve card 2 (Aweil) | Commit the decision | Same flow | pass | pass | **fail** | **fail** | Same 409. Response body: `Cannot transition from dispatched to approved` | blocker |
| 2.6 | Approve card 3 (Turkana) | Commit the decision | Same flow | pass | pass | pass | **fail** | Succeeded. Result text: `Approved. Pre-agreed finance is released for Flood Watch: High Precipitation.` — no district, no amount. Decision log reads `✓ Approved by anonymous` | major |

**What blocked me, exactly.** Two of the three pending cards cannot be approved at all. `GET /api/v1/workflows` shows the three `anticipatory_alert` instances as `wf-aa-1` (Turkana, `focal_point_review`), `wf-aa-2` (Bor, `dispatched`), `wf-aa-3` (Aweil, `closed`). The pending-review list surfaces all three; only the one in `focal_point_review` is transitionable. I confirmed this by calling the transition endpoint directly for each id — `wf-aa-1` accepts `to`, `wf-aa-2` and `wf-aa-3` reject with 409. I worked around it by approving the one card that works and reading the rest as un-actionable.

**Attribution.** Every approval on this deployment is recorded as `Approved by anonymous` in `Recent decisions`, next to two seeded entries reading `Approved by Nyabuot Chan` and `Approved by Peter Deng`. A focal-point decision log that cannot distinguish a real approver from an anonymous one is not an audit trail. (Mechanism, from reading `src/server.js:3160-3175`: the actor is taken from `req.__auth?.subject`, which is absent on an unauthenticated deployment.)

### Recorded as working

- **Task 5's delta framing is the model for the rest of the product.** `Delta is the change in the mean of an uncalibrated 0-100 sensitivity score, in score points. It is not a percentage, not a probability, and not a forecast … carries calibrated_uncertainty: false. Averaged over 16 region scores.` Names the unit, denies the three misreadings, discloses its own calibration state, and states the sample size. The scenario engine was also deterministic across six repeat runs and monotonic across five multiplier values.
- **Task 4's PDF** states its period, generation timestamp, data gaps, and the signal-to-dispatch caveat in full, and degrades to `not measured` with an explicit `Data gaps:` list for an empty quarter rather than printing zeros. This is what Task 1's count line and Task 2's approval modal should do.
- **Task 3's demographics step** — `Used in aggregate only, never linked to your name. Leaving these blank is fine.` — correctly tells the user optionality without pressuring them, and the progress dots advance accurately across all five steps.

### Blocked or worked around during the walk

- **Task 2, steps 2.4–2.5.** Two of three approvals fail with HTTP 409 and no UI path exists to make them succeed. I confirmed the cause by reading `GET /api/v1/workflows` and by calling the transition endpoint directly for all three instance ids. I completed the step by approving the one card that works.
- **Task 3, step 3.5.** Geolocation is never granted in this headless Chrome, so the `Auto-detect` success path could not be exercised. I verified the finding that matters independently — `Here` writes `latitude: null, longitude: null` while recording `source: "reported_here"` — by reading the queued payload out of IndexedDB.
- **Task 3, step 3.7.** To prove the banner was rendered but occluded, I set `visibility:hidden` on the navbar and re-screenshotted. That is a diagnostic override, not a product state; the defect is present without it.
- **Environment note.** Another process shares the Chrome on `:9333`. I opened a dedicated tab per task via `/json/new` and closed it afterwards, so no interference is reflected in these findings.
